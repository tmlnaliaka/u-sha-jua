# Administrator response operations

**Mode:** Operate

**Audience:** Emergency-operations administrators coordinating reports and response across Kenya and East Africa.

**Task and action:** Scan open community reports, distinguish urgency from verification, inspect evidence and location, then confirm or reject and dispatch responders. Review GDACS and Kenya Meteorological Department warnings separately.

**Proof and content:** Current report state, source and age for public alerts, evidence and AI notes with their limitations, map context, and response guidance only after dispatcher confirmation.

**Constraints:** Survivor accounts and admin operations remain separate. Public warnings never verify community reports. The OpenStreetMap base map works without optional keys. Operators may use small screens and constrained bandwidth; controls need clear state and keyboard focus.

**Chosen direction:** The Field Atlas Ledger: cool daylight field-paper surfaces, ink-dark labels, survey blue, and restrained urgency colors. The overview leads with incident counts and a report queue; map inspection is a dedicated map-first page with a side inspector on desktop and a vertically scrollable map/queue on mobile.

**Memorable moment:** Selecting a report keeps its provenance, evidence, verification state, safe response guidance, and dispatch actions together beside its geographic context.

**Unresolved decisions:** None for this surface.
